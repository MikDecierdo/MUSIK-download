Videos / Offline video player

Your videos, offline.

A video player for the videos already on your phone. Browse by folder, pick up where you left off, and watch in a clean full-screen player you control with a swipe. No accounts, no ads, no streaming.

01 / Features

Everything in one small app

Your videos, your way.

Videos finds every video on your phone and keeps it the way you saved it, with the controls you expect from a good player.

01

Folder-first library

Camera, Download, WhatsApp Video: your videos by folder, just as they sit on your phone, plus one list of everything you can sort.

02

Continue watching

Every video picks up where you left off, with Start over one tap away. Finished ones are marked Watched.

03

Full-screen player

Turns to landscape or portrait to match the video. Fit, Fill or Stretch, speed from 0.5× to 2×, and a lock for the controls.

04

Swipe gestures

Double-tap to skip back or ahead. Swipe up and down for brightness and volume, sideways to scrub through the video.

05

Plays the whole folder

The next video starts on its own when one ends. Or tap a video in your file manager and choose “Open with Videos”. Long-press a video to delete it, with a confirm first.

06

Private & offline

No accounts, no ads and no tracking. Videos only goes online to check for its own updates.

04 / Privacy

Nothing leaves your phone

Your videos stay yours.

Videos plays video files that are already on your phone. It doesn't stream, download or share videos.

  1. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no personal data collected. Settings and watch history stay on your phone.
  2. Videos only goes online to check GitHub for updates: once a day if “Check automatically” is on, or when you tap Check for updates.
  3. Videos deletes a video only when you choose Delete and confirm it. Deleted files can't be brought back.
  4. The brightness gesture only dims or brightens the player; the volume gesture sets your phone's media volume.
  5. Keep to videos you made, videos that are free to use, or videos you have permission to have. Videos doesn't unlock paid or copy-protected content.
