MUSIK / Offline music player

Your music, offline.

A music player for the songs already on your phone. Browse by folder, song, artist or album, watch a visualizer that moves with the beat, or relax with a calm beach scene. No accounts, no ads, no streaming.

01 / Features

Everything in one small app

Your library, your way.

MUSIK finds every song on your phone and keeps it the way you saved it, with a few nice extras for listening.

01

Folder-first library

Your songs by folder, just as they sit on your phone, plus Songs, Artists, Albums and Genres. New downloads show up on their own.

02

Beat-reactive visualizer

Neon rings flow around the cover art and move with the bass, the beat and every instrument.

03

Calm beach scene

A hand-drawn beach under the palms that follows the time of day, from a sunrise stroll to a glowing barbecue at night.

04

AutoMix

DJ-style transitions: MUSIK matches the tempo and the beats, then blends one song smoothly into the next.

05

Equalizer & sleep timer

A 5-band equalizer with presets and bass boost, a play queue, and a sleep timer that fades the music out.

06

Private & offline

No accounts, no ads and no tracking. MUSIK only goes online to check for its own updates.

02 / How it works

Three steps

Install, allow, play.

01

Install MUSIK

Download the APK below and open it on your phone.

02

Allow your music

Android asks once if MUSIK may read your audio files. That's all it reads.

03

Press play

Shuffle everything, open a folder, or pick “Open with MUSIK” from your file manager.

MUSIK on your phone.

04 / Privacy

Nothing leaves your phone

Your music stays yours.

MUSIK plays audio files that are already on your phone. It doesn't stream, download or share music.

  1. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no personal data collected. Settings stay on your phone.
  2. MUSIK only goes online to check GitHub for updates: once a day if “Check automatically” is on, or when you tap Check for updates.
  3. The visualizer reads only the sound MUSIK itself is playing. The microphone is never used, even though Android lists visualizers under audio recording.
  4. Keep to music you own, music that's free to use, or music you have permission to have. MUSIK doesn't unlock paid or copy-protected content.
